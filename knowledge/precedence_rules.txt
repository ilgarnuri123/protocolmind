ORDER OF PRECEDENCE IN DIPLOMATIC PROTOCOL

Order of precedence defines the hierarchy of officials during official events.

Typical order of precedence in state events:

1. Head of State
2. Head of Government
3. Speaker of Parliament
4. Ministers
5. Deputy Ministers
6. Members of Parliament
7. Ambassadors
8. Heads of delegations
9. Senior officials

Principles:

- Host has the central or dominant position.
- Guest of honour is placed to the right of the host.
- Seating alternates host and guest sides when possible.
- Symmetry and balance are important.
- Precedence determines speaking order, seating, and procession order.

In parliamentary diplomacy:

- The Speaker usually receives the highest protocol status.
- Delegation leaders sit opposite each other.
- Committee chairs may follow members of parliament in precedence.
