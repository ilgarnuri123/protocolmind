Official Visit Protocol

Official visits normally follow a structured program.

The program may include:

arrival ceremony
greeting line
bilateral meeting
press statements
official lunch or dinner
cultural visit
departure ceremony

Protocol services must prepare a detailed schedule.

Security services coordinate movement and protection.

Motorcades must follow planned routes.

Gift exchanges often occur during official meetings or dinners.

Media interactions should be carefully coordinated.

Protocol officers supervise timing and ceremonial details.
