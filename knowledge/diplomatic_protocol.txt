Diplomatic Protocol Core Principles

Protocol ensures respect for state dignity and official rank.

In bilateral meetings, delegations should sit symmetrically.

Heads of delegation normally occupy central positions opposite each other.

Flags must be displayed with equal dignity.

Official visits normally follow this sequence:

arrival ceremony
greeting line
bilateral meeting
press interaction
official meal
departure ceremony

Greeting lines must follow the order of precedence.

Titles must always be verified before official use.

Protocol services must coordinate with security services during official visits.

Interpreters should sit near principals so both sides can hear translation clearly.
