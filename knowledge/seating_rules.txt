Diplomatic Seating Guidelines

1. Bilateral meetings normally follow mirror symmetry.

2. Heads of delegation occupy central seats.

3. Senior advisers sit closest to principals.

4. Interpreters sit beside principals or slightly behind.

5. Tables may be rectangular, round, or U-shaped depending on meeting format.

6. Nameplates should be clearly visible.

7. Flags should appear behind principals or between delegations.
