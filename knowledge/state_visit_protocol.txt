STATE VISIT PROTOCOL

A state visit is the highest level of official visit between countries.

Typical structure:

Arrival Ceremony
Official Greeting
National Anthems
Inspection of Honour Guard

Official Meeting
Meeting between Heads of State or Speakers

Delegation Meeting
Expanded bilateral talks

Official Lunch or State Dinner

Signing Ceremony

Press Statements

Cultural Program

Departure Ceremony

Important protocol considerations:

- Flag display
- Seating arrangement
- Order of speeches
- Exchange of gifts
- Press coverage
