Seating Protocol Principles

Seating arrangements must reflect equality and respect between delegations.

In bilateral meetings delegations normally sit opposite each other.

Heads of delegation occupy the central seats.

Senior advisers sit closest to the principal.

Interpreters usually sit beside or slightly behind principals.

Tables may be rectangular, round or U-shaped depending on the format of the meeting.

Nameplates should be clearly visible.

Delegations should mirror each other to maintain symmetry.

In sensitive negotiations balanced seating arrangements are essential to maintain diplomatic neutrality.
