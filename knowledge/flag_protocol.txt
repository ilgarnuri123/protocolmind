FLAG PROTOCOL

Flags represent sovereignty and must follow strict protocol rules.

General rules:

- The host country's flag has priority.
- When two flags are displayed, the host flag is placed on the right side from the host's perspective.
- Flags must be of equal size and height.
- Flags must never touch the ground.
- Damaged flags should not be displayed.

Meeting room flag placement:

Host flag – right side of the host
Guest flag – right side of the guest

Press photo arrangement must ensure both flags are visible.
