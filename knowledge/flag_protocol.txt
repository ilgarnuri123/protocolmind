Flag Protocol Rules

National flags must always be treated with equal dignity.

In bilateral meetings two national flags are normally placed behind the principals or between delegations.

Flags must be of equal size and height.

The host country's flag normally appears to the right from the perspective of the audience.

Indoor flag display should follow the official order of precedence.

Flags must never touch the ground.

Damaged or faded flags must not be used during official events.

In multilateral meetings flags may be displayed alphabetically or according to official precedence rules.

When multiple organizations are present, state flags normally take precedence over institutional flags.
