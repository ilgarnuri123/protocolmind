COMMON PROTOCOL MISTAKES

Frequent mistakes in diplomatic protocol:

Incorrect order of precedence
Improper seating arrangements
Wrong flag placement
Incorrect forms of address
Failure to coordinate arrival procedures
Improper gift exchange timing
Unbalanced delegation seating
Protocol confusion during press events

Prevention:

- Always prepare a protocol checklist
- Confirm delegation composition
- Confirm titles and positions
- Prepare seating charts
- Brief participants in advance
