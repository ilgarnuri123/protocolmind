Diplomatic Titles and Forms of Address

Heads of state are addressed as:
Your Excellency.

Ambassadors are addressed as:
His Excellency or Her Excellency.

Foreign ministers are commonly addressed as:
Minister or Your Excellency depending on protocol traditions.

Speakers of parliament are addressed as:
Mr Speaker or Madam Speaker.

Religious leaders may have special forms of address depending on their rank.

Titles must always be verified before publication in official programs.

Incorrect titles may cause diplomatic embarrassment and must be avoided.

Protocol services must confirm titles with diplomatic missions when necessary.
