DIPLOMATIC TITLES AND FORMS OF ADDRESS

Head of State
Addressed as: Your Excellency

Head of Government
Addressed as: Your Excellency

Speaker of Parliament
Addressed as: Mr Speaker / Madam Speaker

Ambassador
Addressed as: Your Excellency

Minister
Addressed as: Minister or Your Excellency depending on protocol tradition

Members of Parliament
Addressed as: Honourable Member

Titles must always be checked before official meetings.
Incorrect forms of address are considered protocol mistakes.
