Diplomatic Protocol Principles

1. Protocol ensures respect for rank, office, and state dignity.

2. Seating arrangements must respect parity and symmetry.

3. Heads of delegation sit centrally opposite each other in bilateral meetings.

4. In multilateral meetings neutrality is essential.

5. Flag placement must ensure equal dignity between states.

6. Greeting lines follow order of precedence.

7. Titles must always be verified before publication.

8. Protocol services must coordinate closely with security services.

9. Official visits require detailed sequencing including arrival, greeting, meeting, press interaction, and departure.

10. Interpreters must be positioned to hear both sides clearly.
