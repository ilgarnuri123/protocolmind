The Story Behind ProtocolMind

ProtocolMind was created by Ilgar Nuri with the support of ChatGPT capabilities, as a specialized premium AI platform focused on the real professional world of protocol service.

It was created to solve a practical need: to provide a modern digital instrument for drafting official letters, structuring visits, organizing seating logic, building protocol checklists and supporting high-level institutional communication in a faster and more elegant way.

The project was inspired by professional protocol practice and by the vision that the standards of state, parliamentary, diplomatic and corporate protocol should have their own dedicated intelligent platform rather than be treated as ordinary text generation.

A special source of inspiration in the creation of ProtocolMind was Mr. Ilkin Mammadov, Head of the Protocol Division of the Milli Majlis, whose professional example, mentorship and devotion to protocol culture helped shape the spirit behind this initiative.

Protocol is not simply order. It is respect made visible, hierarchy made graceful, and institutional dignity translated into action.
