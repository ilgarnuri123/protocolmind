Story

The story behind ProtocolMind

ProtocolMind was initiated and created by Ilgar Nuri, a protocol professional working within the parliamentary system of the Republic of Azerbaijan. The project emerged from practical professional experience: visit programs, seating arrangements, official correspondence and protocol checklists all require time, structure and strict attention to institutional standards.

ProtocolMind was developed as an intelligent assistant to simplify these processes and help professionals prepare protocol materials faster, more consistently and with greater operational confidence.

The system was created by Ilgar Nuri using the capabilities of modern artificial intelligence tools, including ChatGPT, combining hands-on protocol expertise with advanced AI support.

ProtocolMind is currently presented as one of the first known AI assistants developed in Azerbaijan specifically dedicated to government, parliamentary, diplomatic and corporate protocol work.
